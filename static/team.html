Habits by person

Each person's habits next to the team median, to see where Claude Code could help someone more. They are coaching signals, not a score or a performance measure: a long session can be the right call for big work, and experienced users interrupt more, not less. Tell your team you look at these before collecting exports; each person sees the same figures for themselves on their own Habits tab. Only the two shares with a sourced line are highlighted: Anthropic's /usage flags long context (here, requests over 500k tokens) and cache misses at 10% of recent usage (this tool's cold-cache column counts expiry misses only). Built from the habit columns in schema-7 exports; see docs/usage-health.md.
